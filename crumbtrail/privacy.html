Privacy Policy for Crumbtrail

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Crumbtrail is a recipe saver for iPhone: it saves recipes you share with it, together with where they came from. In short: Crumbtrail does not collect any data. There is no account, no server run by the developer, no analytics and no advertising. Your recipes stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

Information we collect

None. The developer does not receive your recipes, photos, notes, meal plans, cooking history, or any usage or diagnostic data.

Your recipes and iCloud

Recipes, the links and captions they came from, photos, tags, meal plans and your "Made it" history are stored on your iPhone. If you're signed in to iCloud, they sync between your devices through your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), under Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot see your iCloud data. Deleting the app removes the copy on that device; recipes you delete go to Recently Deleted for 30 days and are then removed everywhere.

Sharing a meal plan

If you share your meal plan, the people you invite can see and edit it, including a copy of each planned recipe (its title, photo, ingredients, steps and source link). Sharing uses Apple's iCloud sharing; you can stop sharing or remove people at any time.

What your device fetches

  • Websites you save — your iPhone fetches the page directly from that website to read its recipe, title and photo.
  • YouTube — for videos you save, Crumbtrail uses the YouTube API Services to read the video's title, description and thumbnail. By saving YouTube videos you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of that request is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Video thumbnails are refreshed or removed within 30 days, and you can revoke access at any time in your Google security settings (Crumbtrail does not ask you to sign in to Google).
  • TikTok — for TikTok links, your iPhone asks TikTok's public embed service for the post's caption.

No other third-party services, SDKs or trackers are included.

Photos, Notes and Reminders

Screenshots and photos you choose are read on your iPhone (Apple's Vision text recognition); Crumbtrail only sees the images you pick or share. Notes reach Crumbtrail only when you share them or run a Shortcut that sends them. Grocery lists you send go to your Reminders app on your device.

Purchases

Crumbtrail Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. The developer never sees your payment details or Apple Account; Crumbtrail only asks iOS whether the purchase is active.

Feedback and diagnostics you choose to share

Crumbtrail keeps a diagnostic log on your iPhone (recent import attempts and the links they used, sync events, and crash or hang reports that iOS delivers to the app) to help fix problems. Nothing is sent automatically. If you use Send feedback, you decide what to share: app details (app version, iOS version, device model) and, optionally, a copy of that log, sent through your own email or messaging app. The developer uses anything you send only to answer you and fix problems.

Children

Crumbtrail is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
