To help me fix problems quickly:

  1. In Crumbtrail, open Settings → Send feedback and tap Copy next to app details, then paste them into your email.
  2. If a link or screenshot didn't import, include the link — and, if you're comfortable, attach the diagnostics file from the same screen. It lists recent import attempts and the links they used, sync events and crash reports from iOS.

Common questions

A recipe didn't come through. What now?

Some sites block apps, and Instagram and Facebook don't share captions. Crumbtrail still saves the link and puts it in your Inbox — use Fill in from… to add the creator's recipe page, screenshots, or pasted text.

How do I get my recipes on another device?

Sign in to the same iCloud account on both devices and keep iCloud Drive turned on. Crumbtrail syncs through your private iCloud — there's nothing else to sign in to.

Can I share my meal plan with my family?

Yes. In Plan, tap the share button and send the invite. Everyone you invite can add meals, mark them made and send the grocery list.

Can I take my recipes with me?

Always. Settings → Export all recipes makes a zip of plain JSON files and photos. Crumbtrail also imports Paprika and Mela exports.
