Privacy

Pixel Chess Café · last updated October 7, 2026

The short version

Pixel Chess Café collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

What stays on your device

  • Which puzzles you've solved, skipped, or tried, and which little surprises you've found, kept in a private database on your device so the café can remember you.
  • Your settings (sound, haptics, ambience, reminder time).
  • A small summary for the home-screen widget (today's puzzle and which days you've played), shared only with the widget on the same device.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

The Full Edition is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment. The app only checks, on your device, that the purchase exists so the books can open. We never see your payment details.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, it's scheduled on your device as a local notification. No server is involved.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may pass along anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. That's Apple's opt-in, not ours.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
