Lookerizer

Pick any price, stock count or detail on any web page. Lookerizer keeps an eye on it, keeps the history, and tells you when it changes.

Point and pick

Open a page, turn on picking and tap what you care about. Lookerizer shows exactly what it will record before you save.

Works on modern stores

Pages load in a real browser view, so prices built by JavaScript show up just as they do for you.

History and charts

Every change is kept, with a chart per value. Lookerizer stores changes, not noise.

Alerts that matter

Get notified when a value changes, or when a price drops below the number you set.

Share, paste or screenshot

Send a page from Safari, paste a link, or start from a screenshot: Lookerizer finds the page's name and picture.

iPhone, iPad and Mac

Your watches sync through your own iCloud. On the Mac, Lookerizer can keep checking from the menu bar.

Support

Need help, found a page Lookerizer can't read, or have a feature request? I'd love to hear from you.

Tips: if a value stops updating, open the watch and tap Pick Again: websites change their layout from time to time. Pages that need you to be signed in may show different values than you see in your browser.
