Privacy Policy for Lookerizer

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Lookerizer watches parts of web pages you choose (a price, a stock count, a title) and keeps their history. In short: Lookerizer collects no data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your watches stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Information we collect

None. The developer receives no information about you, the pages you watch, or how you use the app.

Your data

  • On your device: the pages you watch, what you picked on them, the values Lookerizer read over time, and any picture you chose are stored in a database on your device.
  • iCloud: that database syncs through your own private iCloud account provided by Apple, so your watches appear on your other devices. The developer has no access to your iCloud data.
  • Notifications are created on your device when a check finds a change you asked to hear about. They are local notifications; nothing is sent through a server.
  • Sharing to Lookerizer: links and screenshots you share are read on your device. Text in a screenshot is recognized on your device using Apple's Vision framework.

The websites you watch

Lookerizer loads the pages you choose directly from those websites, from your device, just like a web browser. Those websites receive what any browser request carries (such as your IP address and a browser identifier) and their own privacy policies apply. Lookerizer does not share your browsing with anyone else.

Third-party services

  • DuckDuckGo: when you start from a screenshot that doesn't show a page's full address, Lookerizer opens a DuckDuckGo search for the site and the product name it read, so you can pick the right page. The search is sent to DuckDuckGo under its privacy policy.
  • Apple: when you share a link, Lookerizer may use Apple's link preview service to show its title and picture, and iCloud sync uses Apple's CloudKit. Both are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Lookerizer shares no data with any other third party and shows no ads.

Children

Lookerizer collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Because it can open any web page, it is rated for adults on the App Store.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
