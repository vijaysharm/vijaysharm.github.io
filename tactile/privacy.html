Privacy Policy for Tactile

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Tactile is a haptic pattern design tool for iPhone. This policy explains how Tactile handles your information. In short: Tactile does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Tactile does not require an account, does not ask for personal information, and contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party data-collection SDKs.

Your patterns and data

The haptic patterns you create are stored locally on your device. If Tactile offers optional iCloud sync, it uses your own private iCloud account provided by Apple, so your patterns sync across your personal devices. The developer has no access to your iCloud data. Files you choose to export or share (.tactile, .ahap, Swift code) are sent only where you direct them via the system share sheet.

Data shared with third parties

None. Tactile makes no network requests to the developer and integrates no third-party analytics or advertising services.

Children

Tactile does not collect data from anyone, including children, and is suitable for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
