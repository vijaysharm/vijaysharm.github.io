Privacy Policy for Fresh Start

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Fresh Start is a resume builder for Mac, iPad and iPhone. In short: Fresh Start does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Your career history, resumes, cover letters and application records stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Information we collect

None. The developer does not receive your resumes, your career history, the job postings you paste, the files you import, or any usage or diagnostic data.

Where your data is stored

Everything you write is stored in Fresh Start's database on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, Fresh Start keeps your library in sync across your devices using your private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can read; it is handled under Apple's privacy policy. Deleting the app removes the data on that device; you can remove the iCloud copy in your iCloud settings.

Importing and exporting

When you import a PDF, Word file or LinkedIn profile export, Fresh Start reads it on your device. Files you export (PDF, Word, text) go only where you save or share them.

Apple Intelligence

Tailoring, rewording, cover-letter drafting and import use Apple's on-device foundation model. Your text is processed on your device and is not sent to the developer or to any third party.

Agent access (Mac, optional)

If you turn on agent access in Settings, Fresh Start runs a server reachable only from your own Mac (127.0.0.1), so AI tools you run there — such as Claude Code — can read your library and create or update resumes. It is off by default. What those tools do with your data is governed by their own privacy policies.

Notifications

Follow-up and interview reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Purchases

Fresh Start Pro (lifetime or yearly) is purchased through Apple. The developer never sees your payment details or Apple Account; Fresh Start only asks Apple whether Pro is active.

Children

Fresh Start is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
