Privacy Policy for Autolens

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Autolens is a prototype app for searching Canadian dealer car listings, checking whether an asking price is fair, and decoding vehicle identification numbers (VINs). It is distributed only to invited testers through TestFlight. In short: Autolens has no accounts, keeps your saved cars and notes on your iPhone, sends your searches to the data providers that answer them, and — only if you choose to share it — records how you use the app so the developers can improve it. It never sells your data or uses it for advertising.

Information we collect

Activity sharing (optional). When you first open Autolens you can choose to share your activity by entering a name and an invite code. If you do, the app records which screens you open, which buttons you tap, how many data lookups an action used and what they cost, and any errors. A car appears in this record only as its make and body style (for example "Toyota SUV"). The record is tied to the name you chose and an anonymous account the app creates for you, and is visible only to the app's developers. It never includes prices, VINs, listings, photos, your notes, your location, or any API key. If you choose "Continue without sharing", none of this is collected.

Crash reports. If the app crashes, a crash report (device model, iOS version and the technical state of the app) is sent so the problem can be fixed. It is not linked to your identity.

Autolens does not collect your email address or contacts, uses no advertising and does not access the IDFA, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Searches, VINs, camera and location

  • Searches you run (make, model, price range and so on) are sent to MarketCheck, which supplies the listings, prices and market data.
  • VINs you scan or type are sent to the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration's free decoder (NHTSA vPIC) to identify the vehicle, and to MarketCheck if you ask for a market check, price history or factory options.
  • Camera access is used only to read a VIN while you point the camera at it. No images are saved or sent anywhere.
  • Location is used only when you tap "Use my location" to search near you. Your approximate coordinates are then sent to MarketCheck as part of that search, and are not stored by Autolens.
  • Plain-English search runs on your iPhone using Apple Intelligence. What you type there is not sent anywhere until you search with the resulting filters.
  • The vehicle history buttons open the Insurance Bureau of Canada's VIN Verify page or CARFAX Canada's site in a browser, with the VIN copied for you to paste. Autolens does not read or keep what those sites show.

Your data on your device

Saved cars, saved searches, notes, recently viewed cars, your comparison list and your settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are not synced or uploaded, and deleting the app deletes them. The MarketCheck API key you enter is kept in your iPhone's Keychain and is only ever sent to MarketCheck.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics and App Check) processes the optional activity record and crash reports described above, under the Firebase privacy and security information and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • MarketCheck receives the searches, VINs and (if you use it) the location described above, under MarketCheck's privacy policy.
  • NHTSA vPIC, a U.S. government service, receives VINs you decode. It requires no account and receives nothing else.

Autolens shares no data with anyone else and shows no ads.

How long data is kept

The optional activity record is kept only for the duration of the prototype and is then deleted. You can ask for your record to be deleted sooner at any time.

Children

Autolens is intended for adults shopping for cars and is not directed at children.
